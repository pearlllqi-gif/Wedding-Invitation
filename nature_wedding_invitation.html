A lifelong partnership, happily established
Pearl Qi† and James Li†
†These authors contributed equally to this work.
Published online: May 2026
Abstract
Love has been extensively studied, yet its most stable form remains lifelong partnership. Here we report the successful formation of a committed union following several years of interaction. At this stage, both parties exhibited strong binding affinity and a negligible dissociation rate. These findings invite you to witness the official ceremony.

Background

This story began during our undergraduate years at UC Irvine, where we happened to work in the same lab. What started as a simple overlap turned into something much more meaningful. Somewhere between the failed experiments, we found something that actually worked.

Methods

Our first real interaction dates back to January 2022. From that point on, going to the lab somehow became much more motivating. We talked a lot, shared meals, took classes together, and eventually made our way to Disneyland (Figure 2a, 2b).

Not long after, James graduated and started his master’s at USC. Still, he made the drive back to Irvine twice a week without fail. Looking back, that period probably cost more in gas than either of us would like to admit, but it also made everything feel worth it.

Somewhere along the way, Pearl managed to turn James, who used to be very much a homebody(宅), into someone who now happily travels around the world with her (figure 2d and 2e).

In 2023, Pearl also moved to Los Angeles to begin her PhD at USC. With the distance finally gone, we decided to move into an apartment together. Around the same time, James quietly became a remarkably capable, all-in-one partner（全能男友）.

Over the following year, life settled into something unexpectedly easy. Pearl found herself no longer needing to worry about much at all, which, for her, was a new experience.

Since fall 2024, we transitioned into a long-distance mode. We logged over 100,000 miles in flights, along with an extensive number of video calls. The distance, as it turned out, did not change things much.

Results

On May 18, 2025, in Florence, a key milestone occurred.

After years of steady progression, what had been building quietly over time was finally said out loud. That day, James asked, and Pearl said yes.

Since then, everything has felt a little more certain.

Looking back, it was never one big moment, but many small ones that brought us here.

And now, we are excited to take the next step.

We warmly invite you to join us as we celebrate our wedding.
