A lifelong partnership, happily established
Pearl Qi† & James Li†
†These authors contributed equally to this work.
Abstract
Love has been extensively studied, yet its most stable form remains lifelong partnership. Here we report the successful formation of a committed union following several years of interaction. At this stage, both parties exhibited strong binding affinity and a negligible dissociation rate. These findings invite you to witness the official ceremony.

Background

This story began during our undergraduate years at UC Irvine, where we happened to work in the same lab. What started as a simple overlap turned into something much more meaningful. Somewhere between the failed experiments, we found something that actually worked.

Methods

Our first real interaction dates back to January 2022. From that point on, going to the lab somehow became much more motivating. We talked a lot, shared meals, took classes together, and eventually made our way to Disneyland.

Not long after, James graduated and started his master’s at USC. Still, he made the drive back to Irvine twice a week without fail. Looking back, that period probably cost more in gas than either of us would like to admit.

Somewhere along the way, Pearl managed to turn James into someone who now happily travels around the world with her.

In 2023, Pearl also moved to Los Angeles to begin her PhD at USC. With the distance finally gone, we decided to move in together.

Since fall 2024, we transitioned into long-distance. We logged over 100,000 miles in flights, along with an extensive number of video calls.

Results

On May 18, 2025, in Florence, a key milestone occurred.

That day, James asked, and Pearl said yes.

Since then, everything has felt a little more certain.

We warmly invite you to join us as we celebrate our wedding.
