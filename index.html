A lifelong partnership, happily established
Pearl Qi1,2† & James Li1,2,3†
1 Department of Chemical Engineering and Molecular Biology, University of California, Irvine
2 Mork Family Department of Chemical Engineering and Materials Science, University of Southern California
3 Department of Biomedical Engineering, New York University, New York

†These authors contributed equally to this work.
Abstract
Love has been extensively studied, yet its most stable form remains lifelong partnership. Here we report the successful formation of a committed union following several years of interaction. At this stage, both parties exhibited strong binding affinity and a negligible dissociation rate. These findings invite you to witness the official ceremony.

Background

This story began during our undergraduate years at UC Irvine, where we happened to work in the same lab. What started as a simple overlap turned into something much more meaningful. Somewhere between the failed experiments, we found something that actually worked.

Methods

Their first real interaction dates back to January 2022. After a lab dinner, James asked Pearl if he could get a ride back home for no reason (well, other than the obvious one). After he got back home, James found another reason to talk to Pearl by offering help to a homework assignment due that same night. From that point on, they talked more and more (Figure 2a). Soon there came shared meals, classes together, and eventually their first date. Gradually, going to the lab became much more motivating.

Not long after, James graduated and started his master’s at USC. Still, he made the drive back to Irvine twice a week without fail. Looking back, that period probably cost more in gas than either of us would like to admit.

Somewhere along the way, Pearl managed to turn James from someone who hates travel, into a happy companion who travels around the world with her (and take pictures for her). Their very first trip together was going to Florida to visit Disneyworld (Figure 2b). There is something magical about being at the happiest place on earth with the person you loved.

In 2023, Pearl also moved to Los Angeles to begin her PhD at USC. With the distance finally gone, we decided to move in together. Over the following year, life settled into something unexpectedly easy. Pearl found herself no longer needing to worry about much at all, which, for her, was a new experience.

Since fall 2024, the relationship transitioned into a long-distance phase. Although both of them were nervous when long-distance first began, things slowly became easier than expected. As it turned out, the distance and time difference did not change things very much. Together, they logged over 100,000 miles in flights, along with an extensive number of video calls.

Results

On May 18, 2025, in Florence, a key milestone occurred.

That day, Pearl had originally arranged for a photographer simply to take some photos for them. James planned the proposal quietly without even telling the photographer beforehand. Maybe that was what made the moment feel so real. When Pearl turned around, she immediately burst into tears. She could barely hear what James was saying, but the answer was always going to be yes.

Since then, everything has felt much more certain.

During our most recent trip, we decided to do our first look a little early. Pearl tried pretty hard to keep the wedding dress hidden from James beforehand, which turned out to be much harder than expected.

That morning, they stood together on top of the castle just as the sun was beginning to rise. The light fell softly across the stone walls and onto us, and the wind moved gently through everything around us. For a moment, the entire world felt unusually quiet.

When James finally turned around and saw Pearl in her wedding dress for the first time, neither of us really knew what to say. After all the flights, the distance, the late-night calls, and the years that slowly brought us here, it felt almost impossible that this moment was finally real.

We stayed there long after the photos were taken, watching the city wake up beneath us. Everything about that morning felt calm, certain, and somehow exactly right (Figure 4 and 5).

It means so much to us to be able to celebrate this next chapter surrounded by the people who have supported, witnessed, and loved us along the way.

We are excited to invite you to join us as we celebrate our wedding and begin this new phase together.
